# Privacy

Leap is built so your health data stays on your phone.

Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Health Connect sync or the optional AI coach. Both are off until you turn them on.

If you turn on Health Connect, Leap can send your scores out, or read another wearable in, through Health Connect on your phone.

The AI coach is off until you add your own API key. It sends a short summary of your numbers (no raw data) only to the provider you choose. Keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Leap has no accounts, analytics, trackers, or ads.

Leap is not a medical device. Do not use it to diagnose or treat anything.

The app's source code is not public.
